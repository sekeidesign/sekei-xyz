The jobs-to-be-done framing, argued at book length. The core claim is that customers don't buy products, they hire them to make progress in a particular circumstance — and that segmenting by customer attributes hides the thing that actually predicts purchase. Useful as a corrective to persona-driven research, though it labours the milkshake example well past the point it's landed.
